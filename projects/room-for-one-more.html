Gameplay

Game Description

Room For One More is a cozy management puzzle where players arrange furniture to fulfill customer requests within a limited room. Each purchase and placement requires players to consider their remaining space, available budget, and room layout.

Core Loop

Review a Request → Buy and Arrange Furniture → Fulfill the Request → Repeat

Review the customer’s needs and check the available space and budget. Purchase furniture, rotate it, and find a suitable placement. Complete the request and prepare the room for the next customer.

Personal Contributions

I developed the game concept and designed the core mechanics around furniture placement and customer requests. I also worked on balancing the relationship between furniture costs, available budget, and limited room space, with the goal of making both purchasing and placement meaningful decisions.

Design Breakdown — Space and Resource Balancing

The design combines two constraints: space and money. A piece of furniture may be affordable but difficult to fit, while a layout that satisfies one request may leave little flexibility for the next.

The balancing goal was to keep both constraints relevant. Furniture placement should require planning, while purchasing decisions should encourage players to consider what they can afford beyond the current request. Rotation, selling, and replacement provide ways to reconsider a layout as the room’s needs change.
