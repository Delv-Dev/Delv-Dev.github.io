# Butt Pressure

## Project Overview

Butt Pressure is a comedic adventure game about Udin, a student who urgently needs to find a toilet. The player explores the school, faces obstacles, and completes small challenges while a decreasing Poop Meter creates constant pressure.

## Design Goal

The goal was to create a short and memorable experience where comedy and urgency support each other.

The game needed to make a simple objective—finding a toilet—feel increasingly stressful without losing its humorous tone.

## Core Gameplay Loop

**Explore the School → Encounter an Obstacle → Complete a Challenge → Manage Remaining Time → Continue Searching → Reach the Toilet**

The player always understands the final objective, while obstacles and minigames delay progress and increase tension.

## Key Systems

### Poop Meter

The Poop Meter acts as the central pressure system. It gives exploration a time limit and connects otherwise separate challenges to one continuous objective.

Because the meter is always decreasing, even a small obstacle can feel important.

### Exploration and Level Flow

The school environment guides the player through rooms and encounters while maintaining forward movement toward the toilet.

The level flow must provide enough direction to prevent players from becoming completely lost while still allowing the journey to feel like an exploration.

### Obstacles and Minigames

Obstacles interrupt the player’s route and create changes in pacing. Each challenge should delay the player without feeling disconnected from the school environment or the comedic premise.

### Comedy Through Gameplay

The comedy comes from the contrast between an ordinary school environment and Udin’s increasingly urgent situation. The mechanics support the joke by making every delay feel disproportionately serious.

## Challenges and Solutions

### Maintaining Urgency Without Creating Frustration

If the Poop Meter decreases too slowly, the game loses tension. If it decreases too quickly, players may fail before they understand the school layout or challenges.

The timer, travel distance, and challenge duration must therefore be balanced together.

### Keeping the Objective Clear

Exploration can become frustrating if the player does not understand where to go or why they failed.

Environmental guidance, readable objectives, and clear feedback from the Poop Meter help the player make progress while preserving the feeling of urgency.

### Connecting Minigames to the Main Experience

Separate minigames can make the project feel like a collection of unrelated activities.

The challenges should affect the same central pressure system and act as obstacles in Udin’s journey rather than isolated game modes.
